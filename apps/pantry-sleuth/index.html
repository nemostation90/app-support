Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Pantry Sleuth.

Pantry Sleuth is a cozy logic-placement puzzle for iPhone and iPad. You are a clerk at an office for household complaints. Place the pets on a miniature house plan, read the typed clue slips, and work out which pet took the food. There is a daily case and a book of cases, and the game is available in nine languages.

Pantry Sleuth is free to play and shows ads. There are three optional one-time purchases through Apple. None of them is a subscription:

  • Remove Adverts: turns off the adverts in the app.
  • Case packs: additional packs of cases.
  • Complete bundle: the Complete bundle purchase.

Purchases can be restored in the app's Settings. Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy them. If something is still missing, email us.

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support.

There is no account and no sign-in. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them. Your purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored at any time.

  • Ad choices: open the app's Settings and review your choice under privacy choices. The consent form is shown where one applies, such as the EEA and UK.
  • Tracking: you can allow or refuse tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Remove Adverts takes away the ads completely.

We usually reply within 24–48 hours.

Privacy Policy

Pantry Sleuth keeps your game progress on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, and what the ads in it collect.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Who we are

Pantry Sleuth: Cozy Pet Logic (bundle identifier com.pantrysleuth.app) is made by an independent developer. Contact details are at the end of this policy.

Data stored on your device

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Data we collect ourselves

Pantry Sleuth's own code does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we cannot see your progress. The game has no analytics: it includes no analytics SDK and no Firebase. The only data that leaves your device is collected by the Google services described below, and by Apple when you make a purchase.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Pantry Sleuth is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: banners, interstitials, App Open ads and rewarded ads that you choose to watch. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about ad views and taps, and diagnostic data, in order to serve, measure and limit ads.

Pantry Sleuth is not directed to children, and we do not treat it as a child-directed app for advertising. For owners of Remove Adverts, ads are turned off. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Pantry Sleuth asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent. You can review your choice at any time in the app's Settings under privacy choices. If consent is not given where it is required, no ads are requested.

App Tracking Transparency

Pantry Sleuth shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Only if you tap Allow can your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) be used for personalised ads and for tracking across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you still see ads, but they are not personalised using your IDFA. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

Pantry Sleuth offers one-time in-app purchases (Remove Adverts, case packs and the Complete bundle). There are no subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, and we never receive your payment details. You can restore your purchases in the app's Settings.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup, your device backup may include Pantry Sleuth's data. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Pantry Sleuth is rated for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because your game data lives on your device, you control it: deleting the app deletes it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or opt out of its use for advertising, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell personal information. For data held by Google, you can use the in-app and iOS choices described above, or exercise your rights with Google directly. You can also email us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and note the change in the App Store release notes.
