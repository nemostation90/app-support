Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about playing ShuttlePals.

ShuttlePals is a cute 3D badminton game where you play as animal “pals”. You can play the single-player World Tour, a Quick Match, the Daily Challenge or the Weekly Cup, and you can also play an optional online match against another player.

ShuttlePals runs on iPhone and iPad. You do not need an account or a sign-up to play.

Open the Shop and tap Restore, or go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Starter Pack, Cup Pass, look packs, pal packs, Remove Ads and Supporter Pack are one-time purchases and are restored this way.

If something is still missing after restoring, email us at pals_green.1m@icloud.com with the name of the item and roughly when you bought it.

Open Settings. Under controls you can choose Swipe, Auto-hit or Pro. Under camera you can choose Classic, Close or High. The change applies to your next match.

Go to Settings → Replay tutorial.

You need an internet connection for online matches. Everything else in the game can be played on its own.

There is no account and no sign-up. Your game progress is saved on your device. If you delete the game, the saved progress is removed with it, unless your device is restored from a backup that included the game’s data.

Privacy Policy

ShuttlePals has no accounts and we do not sell your data. Here is what the game does and does not collect.

Effective date: 2026-11-23

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • Online matches connect two players directly. There is no chat and there are no profiles. Nothing about the match is stored after it ends.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and usage data to show and measure ads (details below).
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your data.

1. Data stored on your device

ShuttlePals saves your progress and settings (for example Tour progress, unlocked pals and looks, purchases you have made, and options such as controls and camera) on your device. We do not receive a copy of this data. Deleting the game removes it.

2. Online matches

In an online match, your device and your opponent’s device connect to each other directly (peer-to-peer, using WebRTC). A small signalling server is used only to set up that connection. The game has no chat and no player profiles, and nothing about the match is stored after it ends.

Because the two devices connect directly, as with any peer-to-peer connection, each device can technically see the other’s network address while the match is running.

3. Game Center (optional, Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best scores (Tour stars, longest rally, cup trophies and total Power) are sent to Apple’s leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is sent.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

ShuttlePals shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between matches. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • data about how you interact with ads.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google’s consent message before ads are requested. You can change your choice later in the game under Settings → Privacy options. On any device you can turn tracking off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. In-app purchases (Apple)

Purchases (Starter Pack, Cup Pass, seasonal look packs, pal packs, Remove Ads and Supporter Pack) are one-time purchases processed by Apple. Payment details stay with Apple; we do not see your card or Apple ID. The game uses Apple’s purchase information on your device to unlock what you bought and to restore it.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not use an analytics SDK. The only third-party SDK that collects data is the ad SDK described above.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run user accounts, so there is no account profile for us to hold.

7. Retention and your choices

  • On-device data: it stays until you delete the game.
  • Ad data: it is held by Google under its own policy, linked above.
  • Game Center: scores are held by Apple under its policy. You can manage them in your Game Center settings.
  • Ad consent and tracking: change them in Settings → Privacy options, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Questions or requests about your data (including under GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA): email us at the address below.

8. Children

ShuttlePals is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the App Store Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact pals_green.1m@icloud.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version on this page.

10. Contact
