Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Hatpin.

Hatpin is a cozy murder mystery logic game. Each case gives you a cast of suspects and a set of clues, and you deduce who did it, then make your accusation. There is a new daily case, an archive of past cases, and extra case files with their own suspects and places.

Hatpin is free to play and shows ads. There are four optional one-time purchases (none is a subscription):

  • Remove Ads: no banners, interstitials or App Open ads, unlimited hints and every past daily case. It does not include case files.
  • The Saltwick Pier Affair: a case file of 12 seaside cases.
  • Murder at the Picture Palace: a case file of 12 cinema cases.
  • Hattie's Complete Casebook: Remove Ads plus every case file, including future ones.

The first case of each case file is a free sample you can play without buying anything. Buying a single case file does not remove ads elsewhere in the app, but no ads are shown between the cases of a case file you own.

Open Settings → Restore purchases. It restores all four purchases at once. There is also a Restore purchases link on each screen that sells a case file. Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy them. If something is still missing, email us.

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support.

If a clue looks wrong, contradicts another clue, or a case seems to have no valid answer, open Settings → Report a problem. It opens an email draft to us. Please tell us the case title and the clue involved, and which device and iOS version you use. You can also write to us directly using the button below.

There is no account and no sign-in. Your progress, streak and notes are stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them. Your purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored at any time.

  • Ad choices: open Settings → Privacy & ad choices to review the consent form (shown where one applies, such as the EEA and UK).
  • Tracking: you can allow or refuse tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Play statistics: turn off Share anonymous play statistics in the app's Settings to stop analytics collection.

Remove Ads takes away the ads completely.

Still need help?

We usually reply within 24–48 hours.

Privacy Policy

Hatpin keeps your game progress on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, and what the ads and analytics in it collect.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Who we are

Hatpin: Cozy Whodunit Logic (bundle identifier com.hatpinapp.app) is made by an independent developer. Contact details are at the end of this policy.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress is stored only on your device: solved cases, your notes and marks, hints used, your streak and your settings. We do not run servers that receive this data. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Data we collect ourselves

Hatpin's own code does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we cannot see your progress. The only data that leaves your device is collected by the Google services described below, and by Apple when you make a purchase.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Hatpin is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: banners, interstitials, App Open ads and rewarded ads that you choose to watch. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about ad views and taps, and diagnostic data, in order to serve, measure and limit ads. We set the maximum ad content rating to PG and block sensitive ad categories. Ads are never shown on the case brief, the accusation or the verdict screens; the only ad that can appear over your notebook is a rewarded ad that you start yourself.

Hatpin is not directed to children, and we do not treat it as a child-directed app for advertising. For owners of Remove Ads, ads are turned off. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Hatpin asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can review your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. If consent is not given where it is required, no ads are requested.

App Tracking Transparency

After you have played the tutorial case, Hatpin explains its ads and then shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Only if you tap Allow can your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) be used for personalised ads and for tracking across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you still see ads, but they are not personalised using your IDFA. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Hatpin uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used, for example whether the tutorial is finished, how many cases are played, and whether purchases happen. It records events such as a case being opened or solved, a hint being used, and a purchase being made, together with an app-instance identifier, basic device and app information, approximate location from the IP address, and crash and performance diagnostics from Google's SDKs. We do not send names, free text, your notes or any account identifier, and Google signals is switched off. Analytics data is kept for 14 months. Hatpin does not read your advertising identifier for analytics.

You can turn this off at any time with Share anonymous play statistics in the app's Settings.

Purchases

Hatpin offers one-time in-app purchases (Remove Ads, two case files and the Complete Casebook). There are no subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, and we never receive your payment details. The app checks which purchases you own with Apple on your device using StoreKit. When a purchase is made, an event saying that it happened (not who made it) is sent to analytics, unless you have turned analytics off.

Notifications

If you allow them, Hatpin schedules reminders for the daily case on your device. They are local notifications, generated on the device, and not push notifications. We do not send you anything from a server.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup, your device backup may include Hatpin's data. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Hatpin is rated for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because your game data lives on your device, you control it: deleting the app deletes it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or opt out of its use for advertising, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell personal information. For data held by Google, you can use the in-app and iOS choices described above, or exercise your rights with Google directly. You can also email us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and note the change in the App Store release notes.
