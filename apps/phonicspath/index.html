PhonicsPath helps a parent teach a young child to read through short, joyful daily lessons. It follows a proven, parent-led phonics method: the app shows each step — a new sound, blending, word reading, a quick check — while you say the sounds out loud and your child copies you.

This is intentional. Children learn to decode best when a caring adult models each sound precisely. PhonicsPath keeps a clean, distraction-free screen and lets you be the voice — you say "mmm", your child says it back. Privacy Policy

PhonicsPath is built privacy-first — everything you enter stays on your device.

Effective Date: July 3, 2026

Overview

PhonicsPath is built privacy-first. All data you enter stays entirely on your device — we have no servers, no accounts, and no cloud backend. We never see, transmit, or store your data, and we do not share it with any third party.

Data We Collect

The following is stored locally on your device only, using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Your child's first name or nickname (optional, parent-entered)
  • The chosen avatar
  • A gentle daily-lesson length preference
  • Lesson and story progress, and per-sound/per-word mastery used to place the next lesson

None of this is ever transmitted to any external server. There is no analytics, no advertising, no third-party data sharing, and no behavioural profiling.

No Audio Recording

PhonicsPath does not use the microphone and does not record, process, or transmit your child's voice. The child speaks to the parent, not to the app.

In-App Purchase

The optional one-time "Full Reading Path" unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. PhonicsPath never handles or stores any payment information. Your purchase status is verified locally via Apple's StoreKit framework.

Analytics & Tracking

PhonicsPath contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, no attribution tools, and no tracking of any kind. Our PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy file declares NSPrivacyTracking = false with no tracking domains.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

PhonicsPath is designed to be operated by a parent or guardian alongside their child. The app does not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. Purchases and settings are placed behind a grown-ups-only gate. Because all data is stored locally on the device, it is fully controlled by the device owner — no child data is ever transmitted off-device or shared with us or any third party.

iCloud Backup

If you have iCloud Backup enabled in your iOS Settings, your device's local data (including PhonicsPath data) may be included in your iCloud backup. This is controlled entirely by your iOS settings and is not initiated by PhonicsPath. The app itself does not sync to iCloud or any other cloud service.

Data Deletion

You can erase all your data from inside the app: Parent Corner → "Your data" → "Delete all my data." You can also delete the app to remove everything. Because we hold no data on external servers, deletion is complete and irreversible.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will note them in the App Store release notes. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

What PhonicsPath Is

PhonicsPath is an educational app that helps a parent teach a child to read using a structured, parent-led phonics method. The app provides a guided lesson path, sound and blending activities, decodable words and stories, and gentle progress tracking.

PhonicsPath is intentionally silent: the parent says the sounds and the child copies. All content runs entirely on your device. The app does not connect to any server, AI service, or analytics provider.
