Frequently Asked Questions

  • How does the teleprompter work? Paste or type your script, then it scrolls over the live camera while you record. You can adjust speed, font size, opacity, and mirroring, scrub by dragging, and control speed live during recording.
  • Are the effects applied to my video? Yes. Filters, beauty, background, face items, funny warps, and voice effects are baked into the recording as you shoot — what you see is what you get.
  • Where are my videos and scripts stored? Recordings are saved to your device's Photos library. Scripts and settings stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.
  • What does Prompta Pro include? A one-time purchase removes the watermark and unlocks 4K, unlimited recording length, unlimited scripts, all filters and effects, advanced beauty, backgrounds, face items, and presets. No subscription.
  • How do I restore my purchase? Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Prompta Pro is tied to your Apple ID.
  • How do I delete my data? Open Settings → Delete All My Data to erase all scripts, presets, drafts, and preferences from the device. Deleting the app also removes all local data.
  • Does Prompta use my face? Only to place face effects and blur or replace your background, live, on your device. Prompta never stores, uploads, or shares face data. See Face Data.
  • Does Prompta show ads? The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Prompta Pro removes all ads. See Advertising and Tracking.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Prompta is a local-first, private app. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app processes, how face data is handled, and the limited third-party services used for purchases, speech recognition, and advertising.

Information You Provide

  • Scripts you type or paste, teleprompter and capture settings, and saved presets.
  • Video and audio you record using the app, and video drafts you save in the editor.

Scripts, settings, presets, and drafts are stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account system and we do not upload your scripts, videos, or audio to our own servers.

Camera, Microphone, and Photo Library

The app uses the camera and microphone to record your videos and audio, and requests permission to add finished videos to your Photo Library. Recording happens on-device; Prompta does not stream or transmit your camera or microphone to us or any third party. You can manage these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Face Data

What face data the app uses. When you turn on a face item (for example an emoji mask or the "incognito" pixelation), a funny face warp (for example Big Head or Big Eyes), or a background effect (blur or replacement), Prompta uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to analyze the live camera frame or the video you are editing. Face items and face warps use only the position and size of a rectangle around your face (face detection). Background effects use a person outline (silhouette) mask that separates you from the background. Prompta does not use face landmarks, facial geometry, depth or TrueDepth data, ARKit face tracking, or Face ID, and it does not recognize or identify who anyone is. Beauty and color filters apply to the whole image and do not use face detection.

How face data is used. Face data is used solely to draw the effect you chose in the correct place on your own video, in real time. It is not used for identification, authentication, advertising, analytics, profiling, or training any model.

Storage and retention. Face data is processed only in your device's memory, one frame at a time. The face rectangle is held for less than one second (to keep effects smooth between frames) and then discarded. Face data is never written to disk, never saved with your scripts, presets, or drafts, and never included in logs or backups. The only lasting result is the video you choose to record or export, which shows the visual effect as part of the image.

Sharing. Face data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us, to Google AdMob, to Apple, or to any other third party, and it is not sold or shared.

Deletion. Because face data is never stored, there is nothing to delete; it is discarded automatically as each frame is processed. You can turn off face and background effects at any time, and you can delete your drafts and all other in-app data via Settings → Delete All My Data. Videos you saved to Photos can be deleted in the Photos app.

Speech Recognition

Voice-following teleprompter scrolling, dictation, and smart cleanup use Apple's speech recognition strictly on your device. Auto-captions prefer on-device recognition; if your language has no on-device model or the on-device result is too short, the clip's audio may be sent to Apple's speech recognition service to produce the captions, under Apple's privacy policy. Audio is never sent to us. You can manage speech recognition permission in iOS Settings.

In-App Purchases

Prompta Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process payment, purchase history, and account information according to Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Advertising and Tracking

The free version of Prompta shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Prompta Pro removes all ads. Before ads load, iOS asks whether you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads; if you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown and the advertising identifier is not accessed. Google may collect device information, coarse location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. AdMob has no access to your camera, microphone, face data, recordings, or scripts. You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Prompta does not include any other analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your videos, audio, scripts, or face data with anyone, and we do not use them for advertising or model training. The only third parties involved are Apple (purchases and, where described above, speech recognition) and Google AdMob (ads in the free version).

Data Retention and Deletion

Your scripts, settings, presets, and drafts remain on your device until you delete them. You can erase all in-app data at any time via Settings → Delete All My Data, or by removing the app. Videos you saved to Photos are managed by you in the Photos app. Face data is never retained (see Face Data).

Children's Privacy

Prompta is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
