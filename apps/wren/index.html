Frequently Asked Questions

What does Wren do?

Wren helps you track your baby's day in one calm place: breastfeeding with per-side timers, bottles, pumping, sleep, diapers, solids, medicine (with next-dose reminders), growth and milestones. It shows time since the last feed, live timers on your Lock Screen, daily summaries, weekly trend charts, and on-device "Rhythm" nap-window guidance based on your baby's age and logged sleep.

Do I need an account?

No. Wren has no login and no account. Everything works out of the box and your data stays on your device.

Is my baby's data sent anywhere?

No. By default all of your data stays on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, your log is stored in your own private iCloud (Apple CloudKit) so it can sync across your devices and, optionally, be shared with a second caregiver. There is no other server and no third-party analytics.

Is Wren medical advice?

No. Wren is a personal logging tool for general wellness. It is not a medical, diagnostic, or sleep-training app and does not provide medical advice. Always consult your pediatrician for health decisions.

What can I buy in the app?

Wren is free to use. There is one optional purchase — Wren Lifetime, a one-time (non-subscription) unlock that adds unlimited history, unlimited children, Apple Watch quick-log, second-caregiver sharing, and CSV + PDF export. Widgets and Live Activities are free for everyone.

Why does Wren ask for notifications?

Only if you enable reminders. Wren can send an optional daily catch-up reminder and medication next-dose reminders. Notifications are entirely optional and off until you turn them on. Wren never uses your camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

How do I delete my data?

Open Wren and go to Settings → Delete all my data. This permanently erases every child and log entry stored on the device. Deleting the app also removes everything, and if iCloud sync was on you can remove the app's data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Wren's paywall (Settings → Unlock Wren) and tap "Restore Purchases". Your Lifetime unlock will be restored to this device and any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Wren is private by design. Your data is stored on your device and, only if you choose, in your own private iCloud. We operate no server of our own, have no account system, and never sell or share your data. No ads, no tracking, ever.

1. Information We Collect

Wren does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or login. The information you enter — your children's profiles and the log entries you record (feeds, sleep, diapers, solids, medicine, growth, milestones, notes) — is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store this information on any server we operate.

2. On-Device by Default

All core features — logging, timers, daily summaries, trend charts, and "Rhythm" nap-window guidance — run entirely on your device. Rhythm predictions are computed on-device from your baby's age and your logged sleep; no cloud service or AI provider is involved.

3. Optional iCloud Sync & Caregiver Sharing

If you enable iCloud sync in Settings, your log is stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit, so it can sync across your devices and, optionally, be shared with a second caregiver you invite. This data lives in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We (the developer) have no access to it. iCloud sync is off until you turn it on.

4. Purchases

Wren uses Apple's StoreKit for the optional one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see or store your payment details.

5. No Advertising, No Tracking

Wren contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not use any advertising identifier, we do not track you across apps or websites, and the app does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt because it performs no tracking. The app's privacy manifest declares "Data Not Collected" and no tracking.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit — optional sync and caregiver sharing, only if you enable it; data stays in your private Apple account
  • Apple StoreKit — the optional in-app purchase, managed by Apple

No other third party receives any data from the app.

7. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, camera or photo data, or any account information, and we run no analytics servers.

8. Children's Privacy

Wren is a tool for parents and caregivers to record information about their own children. It is not directed to children as users, and we do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children.

9. Data Deletion & Your Rights

Because your data lives on your device (and, if enabled, your own iCloud), you are in full control. You can erase everything at any time from Settings → Delete all my data, or by deleting the app. Under GDPR and CCPA you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data — with Wren, that data never leaves your own control.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact
