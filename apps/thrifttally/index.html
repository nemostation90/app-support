Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ThriftTally.

ThriftTally is a profit and inventory tracker for resellers on Vinted, eBay, Poshmark, Depop and similar marketplaces. Log what you paid for an item, mark it sold, and ThriftTally subtracts marketplace fees and shipping to show your real profit and ROI. It also tracks expenses and mileage and gives you a tax-year summary.

An informational threshold meter shows how close you are to common tax-reporting thresholds (EU DAC7, US 1099-K, UK). It is a guide only — see the disclaimer above.

Open Settings → Restore Purchases in the app. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy ThriftTally Pro.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose ThriftTally and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds for purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Go to Settings → Delete All My Data in the app, or simply uninstall the app. Your items, photos, sales, expenses and mileage are stored only on your device, so deleting them there removes them completely.

Fee presets are editable defaults. Marketplaces change their fees from time to time, so presets may change or become out of date — check your marketplace's current fees and edit the values in the app whenever you need to.

Yes, ThriftTally supports iPad as well as iPhone.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-03

Summary

ThriftTally has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you record stays on your device.

Data we collect

None of what you record. Your items, photos, sales, expenses and mileage are kept in local storage on your device. We never receive them and cannot see them.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only for scanning a barcode or taking an item photo, and only when you tap the relevant button. Photos stay on your device.

Ads in the free version

The free version of ThriftTally shows Google AdMob banner ads. Google may collect device identifiers (the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP-derived coarse location, and ad interaction and diagnostic data in order to serve and measure ads. Nothing you record in ThriftTally is shared with AdMob. In the EEA and UK you are shown Google's consent form and can change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy Choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy. ThriftTally Pro users see no ads and the ad SDK is not started.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive any payment information.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete All My Data in the app, or uninstall the app, to remove all data from your device.

Children's privacy

ThriftTally is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page.
