Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about MathPath.

MathPath solves math problems step by step and teaches the idea behind each step. Type a problem or scan it from a photo, then open a hint, the method, every step and the answer. A “twin” problem with new numbers lets you check you can do it yourself. MathPath also has 2,000+ short lessons from Grade 1 arithmetic to calculus, timed SAT-style and ACT-style mock exams, a study plan that counts down to your test date, topic tracks, and 15 number puzzles with a daily challenge. It is made for students and adults.

Arithmetic and order of operations, fractions, decimals, percentages (“15% of 80”, “12 is what percent of 48”, increase/decrease by a percent), powers and square roots, linear equations, quadratic equations, simplifying expressions with one letter, GCF, LCM, prime factorization and simplifying fractions. Everything is worked out on your device.

Tap Scan and take or choose a photo. MathPath reads the text on your device with Apple’s Vision framework, shows the lines that look like math, and solves the one you pick. Photos are not stored or uploaded.

Free: every grade and topic lesson with hints and worked answers, the solver’s hint, method and answer for every problem, 5 full step-by-step walkthroughs a day, the daily challenge and the puzzles at Easy. The free version shows Google AdMob banner ads. MathPath Plus removes ads and adds unlimited walkthroughs, Test Prep with mock exams and generated practice tests, the full study plan, Gifted & Talented, the Challenge Zone, every puzzle level, and Insights. Plus is offered as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription (7-day free trial for new subscribers, shareable through Family Sharing) or as a one-time lifetime purchase (no renewal, not shared through Family Sharing).

Restore: You tab → Restore Purchases (this restores subscriptions and the lifetime purchase). Cancel or change a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MathPath, or You → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. The lifetime purchase is paid once, never renews and has nothing to cancel.

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent message asks how your data may be used for ads; you can change your choice later in You → Ad privacy choices. Apple’s tracking prompt is shown to users who say they are 18 or older and lets you decide whether ads may be personalised; if you refuse, you get non-personalised ads. Users who say they are under 13 see no ads, no consent form and no tracking prompt, and users 13 to 17 get non-personalised ads only and no tracking prompt. MathPath works the same whichever you choose, and Plus turns ads off.

Your problems, scans, progress, saved exam attempts, test date, study days, reminder time and settings are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us: MathPath has no accounts and no servers. To erase everything, open You → Delete all data, or delete the app.

Only one optional daily study reminder, and it is off by default. MathPath asks for notification permission only when you switch on “Daily reminder” on the study plan card. The reminder is scheduled on your device; nothing is sent anywhere, and there are no push or marketing notifications.

English, German, Spanish, Portuguese and French. MathPath follows your device language, or you can choose one in You → Language.

Privacy Policy

Your problems, photos and progress stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Plus removes them.

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Overview

MathPath has no accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect, receive or sell the problems you solve, the photos you scan, or your progress. The free version shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. MathPath Plus users (subscription or lifetime) see no ads.

Information Stored on Your Device

MathPath saves the following locally, on your device only:

  • Problems you solved recently and your daily walkthrough count
  • Lesson progress, stars, XP, streaks, puzzle best scores and the answers used for Insights
  • Saved mock exam attempts and generated practice test results
  • Your test date, the study days you choose and your reminder time
  • Your answer to the age question shown at the start
  • Settings: your goal, school level, sound and language

None of this is sent to us. We have no servers and receive nothing from the app.

Camera and Photos

If you choose Scan, MathPath uses the camera or a photo you pick only to read the problem text, using Apple’s on-device Vision framework. The photo is not saved by MathPath and never leaves your device. Photo access is limited to the single picture you choose.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob (banners only, on the main tab screens, never inside lessons, exams or puzzles). To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and Google’s consent tool may collect device identifiers and advertising data (such as the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, product interaction data (such as ad taps and impressions) and diagnostics such as crash and performance data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent message asks for your choices before ads are personalised, and you can change them anytime in You → Ad privacy choices. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown to users who say they are 18 or older and lets you allow or refuse tracking; if you refuse, MathPath requests non-personalised ads. Ads are limited to general-audience content. See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

MathPath Plus is purchased through Apple, as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether Plus is active (subscription or lifetime); we never receive your name, email, Apple ID or payment details.

Notifications

MathPath can show one optional daily study reminder. It is off by default, and the notification permission is requested only when you turn the reminder on. The reminder is scheduled locally on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Age and Audience

MathPath is for students and adults, from arithmetic to calculus. When you start, it asks a neutral age question. If you say you are under 13, MathPath shows no ads, no Google consent form and no tracking prompt, and the app collects nothing from you through ads. If you say you are 13 to 17, you get non-personalised ads only and no tracking prompt. The tracking prompt is shown only to users who say they are 18 or older. Your answer is stored only on your device. If you believe a younger user has seen ads or a prompt in error, contact us and we will help.

Data Deletion

Open You → Delete all data to erase everything MathPath stores, or delete the app. To reset your advertising identifier, use your device’s privacy settings.
