Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about MathPath.

MathPath teaches math through short, interactive lessons on a colorful learning path. It covers every grade from Preschool to Grade 12, plus topic tracks such as Fractions, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics, Calculus, and a Gifted & Talented enrichment track. Each problem has a Hint, a Help mini-lesson that teaches the idea, and a step-by-step Explanation of the answer. A Games tab adds math puzzles and arcade challenges.

Every grade lesson from Preschool to Grade 12 is free forever, including every Hint, Help mini-lesson and step-by-step Explanation, the Daily Challenge, the classic games and the parental time limit. There are no ads, ever.

MathPath Plus is an optional family subscription (monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers). It unlocks Test Prep (the first lesson and Mock Exam 1 of each level stay free), the Gifted & Talented track, Challenge Zone units, the Medium, Hard and Expert levels of the brain games, and Parent insights (weekly report, topics to practice and a mistake review). Purchases are made only in Parent Corner, behind the grown-ups-only gate, and can be shared through Family Sharing.

To restore on a new device, open Parent Corner → MathPath Plus → "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID (or a Family Sharing member's). To cancel, open Parent Corner → "Manage subscription", or go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Tap the "Me" tab, then "Parent Corner." You'll be asked for your parent PIN (or, if you haven't set one, a grown-ups-only question). Parent Corner lets you change the grade, set the daily time limit, change the PIN, pick the language, and manage your data.

In Parent Corner, turn on "Daily time limit" and choose 5–120 minutes. When the time is used up, MathPath shows a "Time's up" screen that only your parent PIN can open. From there you can add 5 or 15 more minutes, or lift the limit for the rest of the day. The timer resets every day.

No — iOS doesn't allow any app to lock the device or close itself. MathPath's lock screen stops learning inside the app. To keep your child inside MathPath (so they can't switch apps), use Apple's Guided Access: Parent Corner → "Lock the screen (Guided Access)" walks you through it.

On the PIN screen, tap "Forgot PIN?" Answer the grown-ups-only question, then choose a new 4-digit PIN. Your child's progress is kept.

Only on your device. Your child's name, chosen grade, settings, and progress are saved locally. MathPath has no accounts, no servers, and never sends your data anywhere. It works fully offline.

Open Parent Corner → "Delete all data." This erases your child's name, settings, PIN, and all progress, and returns MathPath to its first-run setup. Deleting the app also removes everything. Both are complete and cannot be undone.

Privacy Policy

MathPath collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Overview

MathPath is built privacy-first. We have no servers, no accounts, and no cloud backend. We never see, collect, transmit, or store your data, and we do not share it with anyone.

Information Stored on Your Device

To work, MathPath saves the following locally on your device only:

  • Your child's first name or nickname (optional, entered by a parent)
  • The selected grade or topic, language, and sound preference
  • The daily time-limit setting and today's learning time
  • The 4-digit parent PIN, if you set one
  • Lesson progress, stars, XP, streaks, and game best scores
  • Answer history used for Parent insights (daily totals, per-lesson accuracy, and recently missed questions)
  • Whether MathPath Plus is active on this device

None of this ever leaves your device. There is no analytics, no advertising, no third-party data sharing, and no profiling.

Network Access

MathPath does not connect to our own or any third-party servers. All lessons, games, and artwork are bundled inside the app. The only network activity is Apple's App Store checking MathPath Plus prices and subscription status through Apple's StoreKit. The only web links (Privacy Policy, Terms, and Support) sit inside Parent Corner, behind the grown-ups-only gate, and open in your browser.

Purchases, Ads, and Tracking

MathPath Plus is purchased through Apple. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether a subscription is active; we never receive your name, email, Apple ID, or payment details. MathPath contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. Its PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false, no tracking domains, and no collected data types.

Permissions

MathPath does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or any other protected data.

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

MathPath is designed for children and is set up by a parent or guardian. We do not collect any personal information from children or anyone else. Settings and external links are placed behind a grown-ups-only gate. Because all data stays on the device, it is fully controlled by the device owner.

iCloud Backup

If iCloud Backup is enabled in your iOS Settings, your device's local data (including MathPath's) may be included in your backup. This is controlled by iOS, not by MathPath. The app itself does not sync to iCloud or any other service.

Data Deletion

You can erase everything from inside the app: Parent Corner → "Delete all data." You can also delete the app. Because we hold no data anywhere else, deletion is complete and irreversible.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and note them in the App Store release notes.
