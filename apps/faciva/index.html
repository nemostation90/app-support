Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do the routines work? Each routine is a short, timed sequence of gentle face-yoga moves. The session player shows an animated demo and step-by-step guidance for every one of the 26 exercises.
  • How does the personal plan work? Three quick questions (focus area, minutes per day and reminder time) recommend a routine and can set one daily reminder. You can redo it any time with “Personalize my plan” in Settings or on the Today tab.
  • Where are my journal photos stored? Progress photos and notes stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.
  • Is this a medical app? No. Faciva is a wellness and relaxation app for personal use only.
  • What is free, and what does Faciva Plus include? The free version includes the free routines and shows ads. Faciva Plus unlocks all routines, the custom routine builder and journal export, and removes ads. It is available as an annual subscription ($19.99 per year in the US; prices vary by region) with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, or as a one-time Lifetime purchase ($29.99 in the US) that unlocks the same features forever.
  • How does the Plus subscription work? Faciva Plus Annual renews automatically every year unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel it any time in your Apple Account settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions). See the Terms of Use for details.
  • Can I try a premium routine without paying? Yes. On a locked routine you can choose to watch a short optional video to unlock that routine for 24 hours. It is never required.
  • What does the Lifetime purchase include? A single one-time payment unlocks everything Faciva Plus includes, with no subscription and no renewal.
  • What does Remove Ads do? Remove Ads is a one-time purchase ($3.99 in the US) that turns off the banner, full-screen and App Open ads. It does not unlock premium routines or other Plus features. The optional unlock-for-24-hours video stays available.
  • How do I restore my purchases? Open Settings inside the app (or the Faciva Plus screen) and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. This restores your Plus subscription, Lifetime, and Remove Ads purchases.
  • Why does Faciva ask about ads and tracking? The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Before showing ads, the app may ask for your consent and for App Tracking Transparency permission. You can decline and still use the app; you will see non-personalized ads. Faciva Plus, Lifetime, and Remove Ads all remove ads.
  • Do I have to rate the app or send feedback? No. Faciva may ask once in a while for a rating through Apple's standard prompt after you finish a routine. You can also use “Rate Faciva” and “Send Feedback” in Settings whenever you like.
  • Can I change the app language? Yes. Faciva is localized into 20 languages, selectable in Settings.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Faciva keeps your journal content on your device. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, and purchases are processed by Apple. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed by the app and by the third-party services used for ads and purchases.

Information You Provide

  • Progress photos you choose to capture or import into the journal.
  • Routine completion logs, streaks, badges, goals, personal-plan answers, reminder times, and notes you enter.

This content is stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account system and we do not upload your photos or notes to our own server.

Camera and Photo Library

The app asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to take or import a journal photo. You can manage these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

In-App Purchases

Faciva Plus Annual (an auto-renewable subscription), the Lifetime purchase, and Remove Ads are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process payment, purchase history, subscription status, and account information according to Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Advertising, Consent and Tracking

The free version shows Google AdMob banner, interstitial, App Open, and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and process identifiers (such as your device's advertising identifier), approximate location, advertising data, product interaction data, diagnostics, and related data for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. This means the free version does collect some data through its advertising partner, even though we do not collect your photos or notes.

Where required, the app first shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) so you can choose how your data is used for ads, and it requests App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking. You can decline and still use every feature; ads are then non-personalized. You can change your choices at any time in iOS Settings, and your consent choices in the app where offered. Faciva Plus, the Lifetime purchase, and Remove Ads all remove ads; once ads are removed, the app no longer loads AdMob ads.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Faciva schedules local notifications on your device: one daily reminder at the time you choose and, if you start a free trial in the app, a reminder shortly before it ends. No push server is involved. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal photos or notes. We do not use your photos for medical diagnosis, model training, or advertising. Data processed by Google AdMob and Apple is handled under their own privacy policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your in-app photos, logs, streaks, and notes remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete individual journal entries at any time from within the app.

Wellness Disclaimer

Faciva provides general wellness and relaxation routines for personal use. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Stop any exercise that causes pain or discomfort and consult a qualified healthcare professional with questions about a medical condition.
