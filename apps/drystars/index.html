Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about DryStars.

DryStars helps parents support a child who wets the bed. Each morning you log the night as dry, wet, or "woke & went". In the evening your child earns stars for the bedtime routine. A simple day diary records drinks and toilet trips, and the Progress tab shows streaks, trends and patterns.

Learn articles explain the common causes and what helps, based on published guidance from NICE, the NHS, the American Academy of Pediatrics and the International Children's Continence Society.

Children can't control whether a night is dry. NICE and NHS guidance recommend rewarding things a child can do — drinking well during the day, using the toilet before bed, getting up to pee — rather than dry nights. DryStars follows that approach, so wet nights never cost stars.

Free: one child, the morning check-in, star chart and rewards, bedtime routine, day diary, reminders, the last 14 nights of progress, and every Learn article.

DryStars Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription). It adds multiple children, full history and the monthly calendar, weekly trends, alarm and diary insights, the PDF/CSV doctor report, and logging any past night. Family Sharing is supported.

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the app, or tap Restore on the DryStars Pro screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it (or a Family Sharing member's Apple ID).

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support.

All reminders are off until you turn them on in Settings. They are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone: morning check-in, last drink of the day, bedtime routine, and an optional planned night wake-up. The NHS doesn't recommend routinely waking children at night, so that one is only for families whose plan includes it.

With DryStars Pro, open Progress → Doctor report, choose 14, 28 or 90 nights, and share the PDF or CSV using the iOS share sheet. The report is created on your device and only leaves it when you share it.

Everything is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. To delete one child, open Settings → Children, choose the child, and tap Delete. To erase everything, use Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes all its data.

You can ask a doctor or nurse for help with bedwetting at any age. Book a visit sooner if your child:

  • starts wetting again after being dry for more than 6 months
  • also has accidents during the day
  • has pain or burning when peeing, or pee that looks pink, red or cloudy
  • is very thirsty, pees a lot more than usual, or is losing weight
  • is often constipated
  • snores loudly or seems to stop breathing during sleep
  • is upset or bothered by the wetting

If your child seems very unwell, get urgent medical help.

Privacy Policy

DryStars is built to keep your family's information private. This policy explains what the app stores and what it doesn't.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your device: your child's first name or nickname, age, avatar, chosen approach, nightly logs, day diary (drinks, toilet trips, routine steps), rewards, and notes. We do not run servers that receive this data, and there is no account to create.

Data we collect

None. DryStars has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see your data. The app's privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data.

Purchases

The optional DryStars Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device using StoreKit.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are off until you turn them on, and no reminder content is sent anywhere.

Reports and exports

PDF and CSV reports are created on your device. They only leave it if you choose to share them, for example with your child's doctor, using the system share sheet.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup, your device backup may include DryStars data. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy. DryStars itself does not sync to any cloud service.

Children's privacy

DryStars is designed for parents and caregivers. It does not collect personal information from children, and nothing entered about a child is transmitted to us or to third parties.

Deleting your data

You can delete a child, or all data, at any time in Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and note the change in the App Store release notes.
