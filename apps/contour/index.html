Frequently Asked Questions

What does Contour do?

Contour is a private body-transformation tracker. Take progress photos, log measurements like weight and waist, line up each shot with a ghost overlay, then compare two dates with a draggable before/after slider and watch your trends in clean charts.

Do I need an account?

No. Contour has no account, no login, and no server. Everything you enter lives on your device. There is no social feed and nothing to sign up for — open the app and start.

Are there ads or a subscription?

Neither. Contour is deliberately the honest opposite of scam-subscription body and beauty apps. It is a one-time purchase to unlock Pro — there are no ads, no subscription, and no auto-renewals to cancel.

Where are my photos stored? Do they leave my device?

Your progress photos and measurements are stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData. They never leave your device except through your own private iCloud, if you choose to use it. We have no server and cannot see your photos or data.

Does my progress sync between my devices?

Yes — privately. If you're signed in to the same Apple ID (iCloud) on your iPhone and iPad, your progress can sync automatically through your own private iCloud. It is visible only to your devices and is never shared with us or anyone else.

What's free, and what's Contour Pro?

Free includes up to 15 progress photos, the full timeline, measurements, trend charts, and the optional Face ID / passcode lock. Contour Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited photos, before/after compare (slider + side-by-side), and export — no subscription, yours forever.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings inside Contour and tap "Restore Purchase". Your Contour Pro unlock will be restored to any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Can I use Contour in my language?

Yes. Contour is available in 20 languages. Tap the globe icon in Settings to switch language instantly — your photos and data are never affected.

How do I keep my progress private?

Turn on the optional App Lock in Settings to require Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode each time Contour opens. Because your data is only on your device (and your own iCloud), there is nothing on a server to leak.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

No data collected No tracking No ads SDK No account

1. The short version

Contour is built to collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no developer server. Your progress photos and measurements stay on your device, and — only if you choose — in your own private iCloud. We cannot see them.

2. Information we collect

We do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email, or contacts. The photos, measurements, notes, and settings you add are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, and — if you enable iCloud — in your own private iCloud (CloudKit private database) that we cannot access. We do not operate servers that store your data.

3. Advertising & tracking

Contour contains no advertising and no tracking. There is no third-party ads SDK, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no SKAdNetwork, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because nothing about you is tracked across apps or websites.

4. Analytics & diagnostics

Contour includes no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not build usage profiles. Apple may provide the developer with anonymous, aggregate crash statistics if you opt in to sharing them with Apple in your device settings; this is controlled entirely by you and Apple, not by Contour.

5. Purchases

The one-time Contour Pro unlock is processed by Apple through StoreKit / the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Contour never receives or stores your payment details.

6. Photos & camera

Contour asks for camera and photo-library access only so you can add progress photos. Those photos are saved to your private timeline on your device. They are not uploaded to us and are not shared with anyone unless you explicitly use the share/export feature yourself.

7. Children's privacy

Contour is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, export, or delete on your behalf. You are always in full control: deleting a photo or measurement in the app, or deleting the app, removes that data from your device. iCloud data can be managed in your device's iCloud settings.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
