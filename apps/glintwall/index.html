Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-03

Glintwall is a wallpaper and widget app for iPhone. This policy explains what data Glintwall uses and what it does not. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Summary

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no profile of you on our servers.
  • Your favorites, history and settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use data about your device. You control this.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Data stored on your device

Glintwall stores your favorites, recently viewed wallpapers, chosen vibes, ad-unlock records, downloaded images and widget settings on your device. It is not sent to us. Widgets read a small amount of this from a shared storage area on your device. You can erase it at any time with Settings > Delete All Data. Because we do not receive it, we cannot access or recover it.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data and have no servers that receive data from you, apart from the content download described below. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Content downloads and your IP address

New wallpapers and packs are downloaded from a content host run on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When your device requests this content, GitHub receives standard request information such as your IP address and device/app request headers and may keep server logs under its own privacy policy (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Glintwall does not combine this with any other data.

Photos

Glintwall asks for permission to add images to your Photos library so you can save wallpapers. It uses add-only access: it cannot read, view or upload your photos or videos.

Ads in the free version

The free version of Glintwall shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use data such as your device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), IP address, and ad interaction information to deliver, personalize and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This happens only when you have made the privacy choices that allow it.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): Where required, Glintwall shows a consent form powered by Google's User Messaging Platform before ads load. You can review or change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy Choices.
  • App Tracking Transparency: On iOS, Glintwall asks whether it may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are non-personalized, and Glintwall works the same. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Pro: Glintwall Pro shows no ads and Pro users are not asked to track.
  • Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. Manage Google ad settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID. Glintwall only checks with Apple whether you have an active Pro purchase.

Children's privacy

Glintwall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us and we will help. Ads are set to general-audience content.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete all Glintwall data from your device with Settings > Delete All Data. This does not remove images you saved to Photos, your Apple purchases (restorable), your iOS tracking setting, or data Google holds under its own privacy policy. You can delete the app at any time. Under laws such as GDPR and CCPA you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data; since we hold none, email us if you want help with a request.

Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS. Content downloads use HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app update notes.
