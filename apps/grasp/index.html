Frequently Asked Questions

What does Grasp do?

Grasp turns money questions into simple, visual answers. Drag a slider and instantly see what happens — how compound interest grows your money, how inflation erodes it, when you'll be debt-free, how much to save for a goal, and where your net worth is heading.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. Grasp never connects to your bank and never asks you to log in. You type in the numbers you want to explore, and everything stays on your device.

Is this financial advice?

No. Grasp is an educational tool. All projections are illustrative estimates based on the figures you enter and assume constant rates. For decisions about your money, consult a qualified professional.

What's included in the free version?

You can use the core simulators and read the first lessons for free. Grasp Premium unlocks every simulator, the complete lesson library, unlimited saved scenarios, and removes ads.

How do I save a scenario?

Adjust the sliders on any simulator and tap the save icon. Saved scenarios appear on your Home screen so you can revisit them anytime.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name → Subscriptions → Grasp, and cancel there. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase?

Go to Settings inside Grasp and tap "Restore Purchases". Your previous subscription or lifetime unlock will be restored to this device.
