Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using SleepPath.

How do Night Mode check-ins work?

Tonight shows your plan for the day. Tap Start night to open Night Mode, a dim, red-shifted screen with large buttons made for one-handed use in the dark. Only some methods use timers: with Graduated Check-Ins, SleepPath times each wait and tells you when a check-in is due, and with the Chair Method it times the gaps between steps. Gentle Presence shows a count-up clock, and Pick-Up / Put-Down has no timer.

Use Asleep, Awake and Check in to log what happens. In the morning you get a summary: time to fall asleep, night wakings and the longest stretch.

Will I get alerts while my phone is locked?

Yes, if notifications are on. Check-in alerts (for methods that use timed check-ins) and bedtime reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, so you can lock your phone during a night. Alerts never show your child's name on the lock screen. If notifications are off, check-ins only alert you while SleepPath is open. If alerts are off, turn them on in iOS Settings › Notifications › SleepPath, or from SleepPath › Settings › Notifications.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open SleepPath › Settings › Restore purchases (also on the paywall). Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then SleepPath. You can also use Manage subscription in SleepPath › Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete all my data?

Open SleepPath › Settings › Data › Delete all data. This permanently removes your child's profile, every night log, your plan, routines and settings from this device. It cannot be undone. Your Premium purchase and your ad and tracking choices are not affected: subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings, ad consent in SleepPath › Settings › Privacy choices (where shown), and tracking permission in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. SleepPath keeps data only on your device, so deleting the app also removes its data.

How do I change my ad and tracking choices?

The free version shows ads on some screens. Where required by law, SleepPath shows Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can review or change it in SleepPath › Settings › Privacy choices. You can also change the iOS tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Everything in SleepPath works the same either way. Premium removes ads.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Free users can tap Report an ad in SleepPath › Settings, or email us directly at pals_green.1m@icloud.com with the subject "SleepPath ad report". Please describe the ad and, if you can, attach a screenshot. We review every report. Ads are never shown in Night Mode or during onboarding.

Is SleepPath medical advice? When should I call a doctor?

No. SleepPath provides general information and tools to help you follow a method you choose. It does not replace your pediatrician. Talk to a doctor before sleep training if your child has a medical condition, was born prematurely, has reflux or breathing difficulties, or if you have any concern about their health or safety.

If you think your child needs urgent help, contact your local emergency number. Always follow safe-sleep guidance: back to sleep, on a firm, flat surface, with nothing else in the crib.

Can I change my sleep method?

Yes, any night. Nothing in SleepPath locks you in. The gentle method, night logging and 7 days of history are always free. Premium unlocks all methods, the full plan, charts, full history and schedules, and removes ads.

Privacy Policy

SleepPath keeps your information on your device.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version

SleepPath keeps your information on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no analytics. The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which can collect certain device information, subject to your consent choices. Premium removes ads. You can delete the information SleepPath stores about you and your child at any time in Settings.

1. What SleepPath stores on your device

When you use SleepPath you may enter or generate: your child's first name (optional), your child's age range, your answers to the onboarding questions, the sleep method and plan, night logs (times asleep and awake, check-ins, wakings, notes), routine checklists, reminder times and app settings. This information is stored only on your device. We do not receive it, we cannot see it and we do not have a server that holds it.

2. What we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or health records. We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising-attribution tools of our own. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Ads (free version)

The free version of SleepPath shows banner ads on the Progress, Learn and History screens. Ads are never shown in Night Mode or during onboarding. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this data to serve ads, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where you consent, to personalise ads. SleepPath does not give Google your child's name, logs or answers.

  • On iOS, SleepPath asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads can still be shown, but they won't be personalised using tracking. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), SleepPath shows Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can review or change your choices in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Your ad consent choice and your tracking permission are separate from the information you enter in SleepPath. Deleting your data in Settings does not reset them. Change ad consent in Settings › Privacy choices (shown where required by law) and tracking permission in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • When you have an active Premium purchase, SleepPath does not request ads.

4. Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card, billing details or Apple ID. We receive only confirmation from Apple that a purchase is active so we can unlock Premium. Apple's privacy practices apply to purchases.

5. Notifications

Check-in alerts and bedtime reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

6. Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup, SleepPath's on-device data may be included in your device backup according to your iOS settings. That backup is controlled by Apple and you.

7. Children's privacy

SleepPath is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Information about your child that you enter stays on your device.

8. How long we keep information, and how to delete it

Information stays on your device until you delete it. To remove your child's profile, night logs, plans, routines, reminders and app settings, open Settings › Data › Delete all data, or delete the app. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your device. Delete all data does not change your Premium purchase, which is held by Apple, and it does not reset your ad and tracking choices. Your ad consent choice is recorded on your device by Google's consent tool and is changed in Settings › Privacy choices (shown where required by law). Your tracking permission is changed in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

9. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf beyond what you can already do in the app. For advertising data held by Google, you can use the controls described in section 3 and the settings in your Google account. If you live where privacy law grants you rights (such as GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you can contact us using the link below and we will tell you what applies.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, tell you inside the app.
