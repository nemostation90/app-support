Frequently Asked Questions

What does Auspica do?

Auspica brings classical Chinese astrology into one calm, modern app: pick auspicious dates (擇日) for weddings, moving, business openings and travel; generate your BaZi (Four Pillars) chart; read the daily Chinese almanac (通勝) with lucky hours and day officers; compare two charts for a 合婚 compatibility reading; and explore your Chinese zodiac and numerology. Everything is computed from the birth details you enter.

Does Auspica use my camera or photos?

No. Auspica works entirely from the birth details you type in. It never uses your camera, never scans your face or hands, and never accesses your photo library, contacts, or location.

Is my birth data sent anywhere?

No. Auspica is 100% offline. Your birth details and every reading are calculated on your device and stay on your device. There is no account, no login, and no server — nothing is uploaded.

Is this real advice?

No. Auspica is for entertainment and cultural interest only. It is not medical, health, psychological, financial, legal, or professional advice. Use your own judgment for real-life decisions.

What can I buy in the app?

Auspica is free to use with ads. Optional purchases include one-time reading packs (1, 5, or 12 readings), a one-time Event Date Reading, an annual Auspica Unlimited subscription, and an Auspica Premium lifetime unlock that removes ads and unlocks everything.

Why do I see ads and a tracking prompt?

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob. On first launch you'll see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow it, ads may be more relevant (personalized); if you decline, you'll still see ads and every feature keeps working. A subscription or the lifetime unlock removes ads entirely.

How do I delete my data?

Open Auspica and go to Settings → Delete My Data. This erases your saved birth profiles and readings from the device. Deleting the app also removes everything.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name → Subscriptions → Auspica, and manage it there. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Auspica's Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". Your previous purchases and subscription will be restored to this device.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Auspica is 100% offline. Your birth details and all readings are computed and stored on your device. We do not operate any server, we have no account system, and we never upload your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Auspica does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or login. The birth details you enter (such as date, time, and place of birth), your saved profiles, and the readings generated from them are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store this information on any server.

2. On-Device Computation

All calculations — auspicious date selection (擇日), BaZi (Four Pillars) charts, the Chinese almanac (通勝), 合婚 compatibility, zodiac, and numerology — happen entirely on your device. Auspica does not use your camera, microphone, health data, contacts, or precise location, and it does not read your photo library.

3. Advertising

Auspica shows ads through Google AdMob (a Google service). To serve ads, AdMob may use a device identifier and standard ad-request data. On first launch you'll be shown Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt: if you allow tracking, you may receive personalized ads; if you decline, ads remain non-personalized and every feature still works. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google AdMob is the only third party that receives any data from the app. See Google's policies at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Subscriptions & Purchases

Auspica uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases and subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see or store your payment details.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising (free tier); may use a device identifier for ads, subject to your ATT choice
  • Apple StoreKit — in-app purchases and subscriptions, managed by Apple

6. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, camera or photo data, health data, or any account information. We have no analytics servers and we do not sell any data to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Auspica is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Data Deletion & Your Rights

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control. You can erase everything at any time from Settings → Delete My Data inside the app, or by deleting the app. Under GDPR and CCPA you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data — with Auspica, that data never leaves your device.
